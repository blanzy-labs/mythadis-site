# Legal and editorial standards — internal

## Public Editorial Standards copy

### What Mythadis Does

Mythadis investigates interesting, questionable and occasionally spectacular claims using research, experimentation, evidence and commentary.

Our content is produced for educational, informational and entertainment purposes.

We may examine, test, critique, parody or comment upon products, services, advertising claims, scientific claims, public statements, companies, organizations and individuals involved in matters we believe are worth investigating.

We make a reasonable effort to distinguish factual findings from opinion, commentary and humour.

### BS Meter

The Mythadis BS Meter is an editorial rating system.

It is not a scientific measurement, legal determination, regulatory finding or allegation of criminal conduct.

A BS Meter score represents Mythadis' assessment of how well a particular claim appears to be supported by the evidence available to us at the time of publication, including published research, known mechanisms, technical documentation, independent sources and, where applicable, our own testing.

A high BS Meter score is criticism of the claim being examined unless we expressly state otherwise.

It does not, by itself, mean that Mythadis is alleging fraud, dishonesty, criminal activity or intentional deception by any individual or organization.

### Experiments & Demonstrations

Experiments and demonstrations shown by Mythadis are conducted for investigative, educational and entertainment purposes.

Testing conditions, equipment, methodology and limitations will be described where reasonably practical so viewers can evaluate the results for themselves.

Unless expressly stated otherwise, Mythadis testing is independent testing and is not accredited laboratory testing, peer-reviewed scientific research, a clinical trial, certification testing or a regulatory determination.

Results may be specific to the product, sample, configuration, environment, methodology or circumstances tested and may not apply universally.

Failure to reproduce a claimed result does not necessarily establish that the result is impossible under every possible condition.

Do not recreate experiments involving electrical, chemical, mechanical, medical, fire, environmental or other hazards without appropriate knowledge, equipment and safety precautions.

### Facts, Opinion & Commentary

Mythadis attempts to identify factual claims separately from our opinions and conclusions.

Measurements, quotations, source documents and observed results are presented as factual material where appropriate.

Commentary, humour, conclusions, verdicts and BS Meter ratings are editorial opinions based upon the evidence available to Mythadis at the time of publication.

Opinions may change when meaningful new evidence becomes available.

### Corrections

Mythadis wants the record to be correct.

If you believe we have made a material factual error, contact us and identify:

1. the specific statement you believe is incorrect;
2. the case, video, article or other content in which it appears;
3. what you believe the correct information to be; and
4. any evidence or primary sources supporting the correction.

We will review substantive correction requests in good faith.

Where credible new information demonstrates that a material factual statement is incorrect, Mythadis may correct, clarify or update the relevant content.

A correction does not necessarily mean that the underlying analysis, opinion or conclusion was incorrect.

### Right of Reply

Companies, organizations and individuals who are the subject of significant Mythadis investigations may submit factual responses, supporting documentation or relevant evidence for consideration.

Mythadis retains editorial control over whether and how responses, corrections and updates are incorporated into our content.

### Professional Advice Disclaimer

Nothing published by Mythadis constitutes medical, legal, financial, investment, engineering, safety or other professional advice.

Viewers and readers should consult an appropriately qualified professional where professional advice is required.

### Trademarks & Third Parties

References to companies, products, services, trademarks and individuals are made for purposes including identification, commentary, criticism, review, research and reporting.

Unless expressly stated otherwise, Mythadis is not affiliated with or endorsed by the companies, organizations or individuals discussed in our content.

### Updates to Published Work

Evidence changes.

Products change.

Claims change.

Sometimes we change our minds.

When meaningful new evidence becomes available, Mythadis may update a case, revise an assessment or publish a follow-up.

The goal is not to defend an old verdict forever.

The goal is to get as close to reality as we reasonably can.

## Correction procedure

1. Preserve incoming correspondence, publication version, sources and associated records; restrict access as appropriate.
2. Record the exact disputed statement, location, requested correction, evidence and received date.
3. Route substantive factual issues to the founder/editorial reviewer; evaluate primary sources and contrary evidence.
4. Route legal triggers/deadlines to the founder for appropriate legal review immediately. Record deadlines; do not improvise public replies or use a template as a substitute for review.
5. Record the decision and reasons. Apply reviewed correction/clarification with a dated note where material; preserve prior versions in Git and relevant media records.
6. Review any response with the appropriate reviewer before sending it. Preserve the response and follow-up evidence.

## Legal escalation triggers

A case should be flagged for legal review before publication when it materially alleges or strongly implies any of the following about an identifiable person or organization:

- fraud;
- criminal activity;
- corruption;
- deliberate deception or dishonesty;
- professional misconduct;
- serious medical or physical harm;
- dangerous or reckless conduct;
- misuse of confidential or leaked information;
- serious allegations involving a private individual;
- claims likely to cause substantial reputational or commercial harm.

Also escalate any incoming correspondence that includes:

- solicitor/lawyer representation;
- defamation/libel claims;
- threatened litigation;
- injunction requests;
- formal demands for removal or retraction;
- preservation notices;
- formal proceedings;
- stated legal response deadlines.

The internal rule is:

**Do not debate legal threats in comments, social media posts or improvised email replies. Preserve the communication and route it for review.**
