# Internal legal response templates

Internal only. Use after review; never auto-send or claim preservation before records are actually preserved.

## A. Ordinary Factual Complaint

Subject: Re: Mythadis content

Thank you for contacting Mythadis.

We take concerns regarding factual accuracy seriously.

To help us review the issue, please identify:

- the specific statement you believe is incorrect;
- where it appears;
- what you believe the correct information to be; and
- any documents, primary sources or other evidence supporting the correction.

We will review substantive correction requests against the source material and evidence associated with the publication.

Regards,

Mythadis

## C. Detailed Request for Particulars

Use only after appropriate internal/legal review.

Subject: Re: Your correspondence

Thank you for your correspondence.

Mythadis takes concerns regarding the accuracy of its published material seriously.

To assist our review, please identify, if not already provided:

- the specific statement or statements you contend are inaccurate or unlawful;
- where each statement appears;
- the factual basis upon which you dispute each statement;
- any documents or evidence you would like us to consider; and
- the specific correction, clarification or other remedy you are requesting.

Nothing in this correspondence constitutes an admission of liability, wrongdoing, inaccuracy or agreement with the assertions contained in your communication, and Mythadis reserves all rights.

If your correspondence constitutes formal legal proceedings, a pre-action communication, or requires a response within a specified legal deadline, please identify that fact together with the applicable deadline and any documents formally served.

Regards,

Mythadis
