# Mythadis Pre-Publication Checklist

## Claim

- [ ] Exact claim is preserved.
- [ ] Original source URL/source material is recorded.
- [ ] Screenshots, source media or archived evidence are retained where practical.
- [ ] The case does not exaggerate or materially rewrite the original claim.

## Evidence

- [ ] Major factual assertions have supporting evidence.
- [ ] Primary sources are preferred where reasonably available.
- [ ] Contrary evidence has been considered rather than ignored.
- [ ] Important uncertainties or limitations are disclosed.
- [ ] Quotations are accurate and presented in context.

## Experiment

- [ ] Methodology is documented.
- [ ] Relevant equipment/configuration is documented.
- [ ] Limitations are identified.
- [ ] Raw results are retained where practical.
- [ ] The conclusion does not claim more than the experiment demonstrates.

## Language

- [ ] Fact is distinguishable from opinion/commentary.
- [ ] The case attacks the claim rather than casually assigning motive.
- [ ] Words such as fraud, scam, criminal, dishonest, corrupt or intentionally deceptive are not used as factual allegations without unusually strong evidence and review.
- [ ] BS Meter score is supportable from the case record.
- [ ] Comedy does not materially distort the underlying facts.

## Right of Reply

For significant allegations involving an identifiable company, organization or individual:

- [ ] Consider whether a pre-publication right of reply is appropriate.
- [ ] Preserve any response received.
- [ ] Represent material responses fairly.
- [ ] Record when no response was received.

## Final

- [ ] Final BS Meter is recorded.
- [ ] Final verdict is supported by the evidence.
- [ ] Sources/notes are preserved.
- [ ] Legal escalation triggers have been checked.
- [ ] Editorial Standards disclaimer renders correctly.


For planned investigations, record final score/verdict as pending; do not invent results to tick a box.
