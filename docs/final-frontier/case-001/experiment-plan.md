## 1. Specific claim

Working subject ONLY: an unspecified AI investment system turning an initial $68 into $6,732 overnight. Do not attribute it to any person/company until the original material is obtained. Establish whether $6,732 means ending balance or profit, whether the promise is typical/guaranteed or merely possible, and whether the currency is USD. The title alone does not answer these questions.

## 2. Falsifiable hypothesis and registration gate

Conditional operational hypothesis: with initial capital 68 units, no further deposits, the source-specified system and rules produce an ending net realizable value of at least 6,732 units by the exact source-defined deadline. Lock market, period/time zone, model/version, instructions, prices, fees, spread, slippage, leverage and failure handling BEFORE examining outcomes. If those details cannot be obtained, the claim remains insufficiently specified; do not substitute a generic chatbot or investment strategy.

This paper test concerns arithmetic/replay feasibility, not demonstrated AI causality or actual withdrawability. A statement that this *can* happen is not falsified by a few failed runs. A universal guarantee can be challenged by a valid in-scope counterexample, but only with all stated conditions preserved. Typical-return claims need representative sampling and an agreed success frequency, not an invented threshold.

## 3. Supporting evidence

Preserved original claim with context; reproducible, timestamped inputs and instructions; complete trade/account ledger including failures and losses; consistent fee/financing accounting; independently checkable price records. A paper replay meeting the threshold supports only simulated feasibility under declared assumptions. Real performance/withdrawability would need independently verifiable existing records, not a dashboard screenshot. Successful outcomes alone do not isolate the contribution of AI.

## 4. Undermining evidence

A valid failure contradicts an unconditional universal guarantee within its defined scope. Missing funds, added deposits, omitted losses/costs, changed deadlines or selected winners undermine an advertised example's interpretation. Missing documentation establishes an evidence gap, not fraud or impossibility. A paper run that misses the threshold establishes only failure for that simulation and assumptions.

## 5. Procedure — proposed, not executed

1. Obtain the original URL/capture and full context from founder. Preserve dated originals and provenance alongside the canonical case evidence; redact personal data from public derivatives. Make no contact with claimants in this slice.
2. Extract exact wording and scope. Confirm claimant attribution and currency. Record unresolved questions rather than filling them in. Founder approves the precise claim and protocol before a test.
3. Desktop arithmetic check: if 6,732 is ending balance, multiplier = 6,732 / 68 = 99; net gain = 6,664; net return = 6,664 / 68 × 100 = 9,800%. This is a mathematical consequence of the example, not an observed return. If 6,732 means profit, recompute; do not reuse these values.
4. Assess published evidence and existing records against the evidence criteria. Regulator guidance is context, not case-specific proof. Document each source's date, relevance and limitations.
5. ONLY if source rules and free lawful price/history records exist: preregister a manual paper replay. Use a spreadsheet or ledger, no broker account, deposits, live orders, paid tools or new automation. Freeze a contiguous set of 10 eligible overnight windows selected before viewing strategy outcomes. This is an exploratory sample, not a statistically powered estimate. If market/rules/time window are unspecified, stop at the source audit.
6. Begin each independent window at 68 units. Follow only the source's frozen strategy. Use prices actually available at decision time; no future information or hindsight prompts. Record every action and a justified execution/cost assumption. Include full losses and financing/liquidation effects where relevant; if reliable inputs are absent, report the replay as not executable.
7. Run the same windows with a cash/no-trade balance of 68 as the simple accounting control. Optionally preregister a fixed buy-and-hold reference ONLY when asset and execution rules are known. Neither control alone isolates AI causality; that would require a matched non-AI strategy and a separate protocol.
8. Preserve input records, ledger and every run. Summarize individual outcomes, successes/denominator, failures, costs, and assumptions. Do not pool away failed windows or present paper values as withdrawn money.
9. Founder/editorial review the conclusion against the exact wording. No final BS rating is assigned by this plan. Store actual future results/evidence alongside this document; the video repo links here instead of copying an evidence store.

## 6. Materials / expenditure

Existing computer, browser, calculator or ordinary spreadsheet, source captures and free accessible price records. Optional existing camera for filming the host or documents. ## 7. Measurements / observations to record

Source wording and date; rules/version; window start/end/time zone; initial balance; timestamps; positions/quantities; price-source identity; fees/spread/slippage/financing; deposits/withdrawals (must be zero in replay); ending marked and net realizable balances; return; drawdown and liquidation where calculable; missing inputs and deviations. Observation fields are currently EMPTY. Do not fabricate plausible-looking trial rows, screenshots or charts.

## 8. Variables / confounders

Market regime, volatility, selected assets/windows, liquidity, bid/ask spreads, leverage, hidden deposits/bonuses, changing model output, prompt changes, undocumented fees, cherry-picking, look-ahead/survivorship bias and fictitious platform balances. Fix what can be fixed; disclose what cannot. Determinism and quoted executions may not be reproducible even with recorded prompts.

## 9. Controls / comparisons

Cash control for the same starting amount and time windows. Preregister any asset reference before observing results. Use identical timing/cost assumptions when comparisons apply. A made-up growing-money animation is a demonstration only; a paper account is a simulation; third-party records are published-evidence analysis. None is a performed real-money Mythadis experiment.

## 10. Limitations / uncertainty

No identified real claim or experimental protocol is approved. No experiment has been performed. Paper execution cannot establish real fills, ability to withdraw, reliability, risk or promoter intent. Ten exploratory windows cannot substantiate typical performance or invalidate an existential possibility. Missing evidence cannot prove deception. A large calculated multiplier cannot establish that a return is impossible. Any stronger conclusion requires additional evidence and review.

## 11. Interpretation rules

- Source missing/ambiguous: fixture only; no named accusation or supported case rating.
- Source available but rules/data missing: unsubstantiated or untestable *on the material available*; disclose limits, no claim of demonstrable falsity.
- Paper threshold met: simulated outcome under explicit assumptions; not verified investment profit or general efficacy.
- Paper threshold missed: report the exact failed conditions; apply the logical distinctions in section 2.
- Independently verifiable existing real records: assess authenticity, completeness and exact scope before any qualified support/refutation.
- Final BS: founder-reviewed editorial judgment with explanation, separate from statistical confidence. Current 9.4 remains a fictional fixture; final score and verdict remain unset.
